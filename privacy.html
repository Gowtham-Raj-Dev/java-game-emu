Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

The short version: JavaPlay has no accounts and never asks for your name or email address. The games you open and your saves stay on your own device.

What we do not collect

  • No sign-up, login or profile, and nothing to pay for.
  • Your .jar files, game saves and settings are not uploaded to us.
  • No contact form, comments or newsletter.

What is stored on your device

The emulator keeps games you open, their save data and your settings (such as keypad layout and sound) in your browser's storage (local storage and IndexedDB), or on your phone in the Android app. It stays on your device; we cannot read it. Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes it.

Analytics

The website uses Google Analytics to count visits — pages viewed, device and browser type, rough location and how you arrived. It sets cookies to do this; we use the figures to see which games are played and to fix problems, not to identify you. You can opt out with Google's opt-out browser add-on. Analytics and ads don't run inside the Android app.

Advertising

We use or may use Google AdSense to show ads on JavaPlay. Where ads are shown:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings, and opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at www.aboutads.info.
  • Google explains how it uses information from sites that use its services.

Hosting and server logs

JavaPlay is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website, the servers that deliver it process technical request data — your IP address, the time, the address requested and your browser type — to send you the pages and keep the service secure, under GitHub's own privacy statement.

The Android app

The app runs games from files on your phone and keeps your saves there. It may check online whether a newer version is available; it sends nothing about you beyond that request. Its built-in browser opens other game sites you choose, which have their own policies.

Links to other sites

JavaPlay links to other Java game sites and to other CodeLove sites. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

JavaPlay is meant for a general audience, needs no personal details, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and changes

You can block or delete cookies and this site's stored data in your browser at any time. If we change how JavaPlay handles data, we'll update this page and the date above. Questions: support@codelove.in.
